Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

Our "0% Data Shared" Clarification: Shoonya Origins provides an ecosystem of 130+ professional intelligence tools. The calculations, inputs, and sensitive data (such as health metrics in Aura or infrastructural data in Construct) processed by these tools run 100% locally on your device. We do not access, store, or transmit this sensitive tool data. However, to maintain and optimize our public-facing website, we utilize standard web tracking scripts (detailed below).

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two distinct categories:

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: When you contact us via our forms, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your communication.
  • Automated Website Tracking Data: When you navigate our website, we automatically collect metadata using third-party scripts (such as Google Tag Manager and Apollo). This includes IP addresses, browser types, interaction metrics, and device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

The tracking data collected on our website is used strictly for internal analytics, improving user experience, and B2B engagement. We do not sell your personal browsing data to third-party data brokers. Your contact information is used solely to respond to inquiries or provide updates regarding the Shoonya Origins ecosystem.

3. Local Processing vs. Server Data

It is critical to distinguish between our website and our tools. Utilizing the 130+ free tools provided by Shoonya Origins (including Aura, Raptor, and Shakti) does not result in the transmission of your sensitive operational data to our servers. All logic and computational decision trees are executed in your local browser environment.

4. Third-Party Services and Cookies

We employ cookies and tracking pixels to analyze website traffic. By using this website, you consent to the processing of data by these third-party analytics providers in the manner and for the purposes set out above. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some website features may be affected.

5. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect the minimal data we store. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.
