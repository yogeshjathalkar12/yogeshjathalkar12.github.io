What

Shoonya Origins is a deep-tech company building technology at the intersection of mathematics and physics: computer vision, physical AI, quantum machine learning, biological modelling and more. We take that thinking into fields where it has rarely been applied, and bring it to the world through focused ventures.

Most tools in a field only record data. We model the system underneath: biology in health, human relationships in sales, physical environments in construction and manufacturing.

Why

We are drawn to problems that are hard, messy and close to the brink of mattering. We believe the technologies that shape the next era of humanity will come from deep mathematical and physical understanding. Software is simply the medium we use to express it.

Coral: Urban Intelligence for Mumbai & Pune

Citizens report, our AI sees, government acts. Coral turns everyday frustrations such as potholes, broken pipes and illegal dumping into structured, anonymous intelligence that civic bodies can act on.

A report takes 15 seconds and your identity stays hidden. Computer vision classifies the issue and its severity and groups it with related reports. Once 15 or more matching reports are in, a scored brief with photographic evidence goes to the civic body. Coral is at the prototyping stage, and we are looking for aware citizens to help us gather intelligence.

  • Detects potholes, illegal dumping, drainage faults and broken infrastructure
  • Anonymous by design: no names or phone numbers leave your device
  • Pattern recognition that predicts where the next breakdown will occur
  • Free civic, safety and environment tools for everyone

Vapus: Your Lifetime Health Vault

Your health has a story, and now it has a home. Vapus is a lifetime vault for every symptom, prescription and scan: logged once, kept forever and read only by the people you choose.

AI works quietly in the background, turning raw entries into early warning instead of just an archive, and vitals can be read from standard video. It is strictly in testing at the prototyping stage: we do not claim it to be a medical device, so do not use it for medical purposes.

  • Timeline, documents and medications in one vault
  • Time-boxed secure links: you approve every share
  • Delegates: a parent or caregiver can log or view for you
  • A 3D body explorer with around 2,800 named parts
