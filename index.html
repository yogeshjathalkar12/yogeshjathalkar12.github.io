Coral: Urban Intelligence for Mumbai & Pune

Citizens report, our AI sees, government acts. Coral turns everyday frustrations such as potholes, broken pipes and illegal dumping into structured, anonymous intelligence that civic bodies can act on.

A report takes 15 seconds and your identity stays hidden. Computer vision classifies the issue and its severity and groups it with related reports. Once 15 or more matching reports are in, a scored brief with photographic evidence goes to the civic body. Coral is at the prototyping stage, and we are looking for aware citizens to help us gather intelligence.

  • Detects potholes, illegal dumping, drainage faults and broken infrastructure
  • Anonymous by design: no names or phone numbers leave your device
  • Pattern recognition that predicts where the next breakdown will occur
  • Free civic, safety and environment tools for everyone

Vapus: Your Lifetime Health Vault

Your health has a story, and now it has a home. Vapus is a lifetime vault for every symptom, prescription and scan: logged once, kept forever and read only by the people you choose.

AI works quietly in the background, turning raw entries into early warning instead of just an archive, and vitals can be read from standard video. It is strictly in testing at the prototyping stage: we do not claim it to be a medical device, so do not use it for medical purposes.

  • Timeline, documents and medications in one vault
  • Time-boxed secure links: you approve every share
  • Delegates: a parent or caregiver can log or view for you
  • A 3D body explorer with around 2,800 named parts

Shakti: Defence Technology That Protects People

Shakti is the defence venture of Shoonya Origins. We build next-generation automated technology for unmanned defence, wearable smart devices for combat environments, and the computing systems that connect them.

Courage is human. The risk shouldn’t have to be. Shakti works on technology that keeps people out of harm’s way and gives those who must be there a clearer picture of what is around them, with a human operator making the final decision.

  • SAMPATI: tethered aerostats that locate radar emitters without relying on satellites
  • VAHAK-AI: a retrofit kit that lets Army heavy trucks drive themselves and keep navigating when satellite signals are jammed
  • ASTRR-SYSTEM: a picture of what is in orbit that does not depend on foreign data
  • NETRA: open-source intelligence analysis that runs on the user’s own computer

Eklavya: Continuous Career Guidance

Eklavya helps students build an evolving profile of their interests, skills and achievements from age 12 through professional life, and uses it to surface the scholarships, colleges, internships and careers that actually fit them.

It is built directly into a real learning platform, so course activity, quiz performance and practice sessions strengthen your profile automatically, not just what you type into a form. Named after the legendary student who reached mastery through dedication, it is made for India’s reality, where most students get career counselling only at a few critical moments.

  • A profile that grows with the student, from age 12 onwards
  • Matches to scholarships, colleges, internships and jobs based on their own profile
  • A simple, conversational way to start building a profile
  • Interest-based communities and a curated database of opportunities

Aether Labs: One Platform for Every Kind of Research

Aether Labs is the research platform of Shoonya Origins. Whatever you study, from physics and biology to engineering, data and the social sciences, Aether gives you one place to do the work, keep it organised and share it with the people who need it.

Research gets slowed down by scattered tools, buried data and people working alone. Aether brings those pieces together: computational tools for analysis, modelling and simulation without expensive licences in the way, a home for datasets and results that are properly cited, and a way to share what you find so the same problem isn’t solved twice in isolation.

  • Tools for analysis, modelling and simulation
  • Datasets, methods and results kept organised and cited
  • Researchers, students and institutions working together
  • Open to any field: physical, life, engineering, computing and social sciences
