SHAKTI

What we do

Shakti is the defence venture of Shoonya Origins. We build technology that keeps people out of harm’s way, and gives the people who must be there a clearer picture of what is around them.

Defence Unmanned systems Wearables Computing
Our work 01

Our work falls into three areas.

01

Unmanned & automated systems

Ground and aerial platforms, and persistent sensing systems, that take on dull, dirty and dangerous work, such as moving supplies and watching difficult borders, so fewer people have to.

02

Wearable smart devices

Compact devices for people in the field, built for combat conditions, that improve awareness and communication when it matters most.

03

Computing systems

Software and computing that turn raw sensor data into clear, usable information where the work is actually happening.

Who we work for 02

India’s defence forces, and the programmes that equip them. Where we can, we build so that a capability does not depend on systems controlled by anyone else.
