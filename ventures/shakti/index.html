Projects 02
Surveillance · Electronic support Design & software stage

SAMPATI

A network of tethered, helium-assisted aerostats that listens for radar emissions along high-altitude borders. By comparing when each node hears the same signal, it works out where the emitter is, without relying on satellites.

Developed against the iDEX Prime challenge on UAS-based electronic support for hilly and high-altitude terrain. The processing and control software is built and verified in simulation; field hardware is the next stage.

Logistics · Autonomous trucks Software stage

VAHAK-AI

A retrofit kit and software stack that lets existing Army heavy trucks drive themselves over rough terrain, keep navigating when satellite signals are jammed, and move as a coordinated convoy.

Proposed against the iDEX ADITI 4.0 challenge on autonomous load carrier trucks. The vehicle software and command dashboard are built; it has not yet been run on a vehicle.

Space domain awareness · Air Force Software stage

ASTRR-SYSTEM

A system that gives the Indian Air Force its own picture of what is in orbit and what is coming down, so space awareness does not depend on foreign data. It warns of collisions, hostile satellite moves, falling debris and missile launches.

Proposed against the iDEX ADITI 4.0 challenge on space domain training, surveillance and operations.

Electronic warfare · Cognitive systems Software stage

IGNIS-COGNITIVE

A software system that learns to recognise unfamiliar radar emitters, uses terrain to understand what they are doing, and recommends a response to a human operator, who always makes the final decision.

Proposed against the iDEX ADITI 4.0 challenge on cognitive electronic warfare. The software is built and tested on simulated data only; testing on real recordings and field hardware are the next stages.

Open-source intelligence · Analysis Prototype stage

NETRA

A desktop system that gathers public news and social media, checks how far each source can be trusted, spots edited or fake images, and shows what is happening, where and when. Every finding points back to the report behind it, and analysts make the final call. It runs entirely on the user’s own computer.

Prepared against the Indian Air Force problem statement on AI-based open-source intelligence analysis and monitoring. Built and tested on simulated data only; trials on live collected material are the next stage.

Quantum sensing · Navy radar Simulation stage

ASTRA-Q

A quantum radar demonstrator that sends out one half of a pair of linked (entangled) photons and keeps the other half at home. Matching the two lets it pick out faint, hard-to-see targets in heavy background noise and barrage jamming. The photon source runs at room temperature.

Proposed against the iDEX ADITI 4.0 challenge on a sovereign quantum radar for the Indian Navy. Every module has been modelled and checked in a physics simulation; the laboratory hardware is the next stage.
