ASTRR-SYSTEM

A space domain awareness system that gives the Indian Air Force its own picture of what is in orbit and what is coming down, so that seeing space does not depend on data from anyone else.

Space domain awareness Air Force Early warning Operator training
The problem 01

India’s view of space relies heavily on tracking data supplied by foreign networks. In a conflict, that feed can be cut, and the picture goes with it.

Even with the data, some things are hard to see: satellites that stay quiet to optical and radar tracking, objects that change course with little warning, and missiles that cross a region in minutes.

What ASTRR-SYSTEM does 02

ASTRR-SYSTEM watches the space above India and tells operators what matters, how serious it is and how long they have to act.

01

Watches the sky above a region

Shows every object passing over an area of interest, so a commander can see what is overhead around a conflict zone or a key asset.

02

Protects Indian satellites

Warns when another object comes close to an Indian satellite, and works out how likely a collision is, so real risks stand apart from false alarms.

03

Spots hostile moves

Notices when a satellite changes its orbit and judges whether the move looks routine or threatening, with a threat rating for each.

04

Identifies unknown objects

Works out what an unidentified object is, including satellites that stay hidden from optical and radar tracking.

05

Warns of falling objects

Predicts where a re-entering object could come down and alerts the sites inside the area at risk.

06

Reacts to missile launches

Recognises ballistic and hypersonic threats within seconds and shows where they are heading.
