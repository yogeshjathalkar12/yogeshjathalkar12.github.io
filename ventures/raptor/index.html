How It Works

The Complete
Loop

Most sales tools are specialists. Raptor is a single brain that owns the entire journey — from knowing nothing about your business to closing deals on your behalf — and compounds its intelligence with every interaction.

01

Feed Raptor data about your company — your product, your values, your ideal client, your competitive edge. The AI neural network builds a deep model of your organisation. This becomes the foundation everything else runs on.

02
Intelligence

Hunt the Market

Raptor searches the internet autonomously — LinkedIn, news, job boards, company databases, social signals — finding your ideal clients wherever they exist. Simultaneously it gathers competitive intelligence and identifies potential partners without you lifting a finger.

03
Classification

Classify Every Prospect

Every prospect is analysed across 100+ parameters — company size, growth signals, technology stack, buying intent, decision maker profiles, budget indicators. Raptor categorises and ranks them by conversion probability so you always know exactly where to focus.

04
Precision

Craft the Perfect Pitch

For each prospect, Raptor generates a personalised pitch and strategy — the right message, the right channel, the right timing. Not a template. A genuinely tailored approach built from everything it knows about that specific person and organisation.

05
Execution

Guide Every Conversation

During live calls, Raptor provides real-time guidance. It automates follow-up messages. It reads sentiment from email replies and voice tone. It tells you when to push, when to wait, when to close — before you even ask.

06
Evolution

Learn. Retune. Repeat.

After every interaction — every call, every reply, every deal won or lost — Raptor analyses what happened, identifies what worked, and retrains itself. The brain gets smarter with every single data point. Permanently. Automatically.
