What exactly is Raptor? +
Raptor is an autonomous AI sales intelligence platform by ShoonyaOrigins. Unlike point solutions, it handles the entire sales loop — from prospect discovery and scoring, to personalised pitch creation, live call guidance, and self-improving learning after every deal. One brain. The whole loop.
How is Raptor different from Apollo, Salesforce, or Outreach? +
Those tools are specialists. Apollo finds leads. Outreach automates sequences. Salesforce manages records. None of them talk to each other. None of them learn. Raptor is one unified brain that owns the entire sales loop — and permanently improves after every interaction, creating a proprietary AI asset no competitor can replicate.
What does "self-retuning" actually mean? +
After every sales interaction — every call, every email reply, every win or loss — Raptor's hybrid LLM + RNN architecture analyses what happened. It reinforces patterns that lead to conversions and discards what failed. The model retrains itself permanently, meaning the longer you use Raptor, the smarter and more valuable it becomes to your specific organisation.
Who is Raptor built for? +
Any B2B organisation with an active sales motion — startups that want a 20-person sales org from day one, mid-size enterprises running 5 disconnected tools, SaaS companies that need personalisation at scale, consultancies with complex relationship-driven cycles, and real estate firms targeting HNI clients with timing precision.
