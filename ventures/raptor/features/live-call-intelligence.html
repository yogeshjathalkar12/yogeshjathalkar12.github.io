Live Call Intelligence

Raptor listens to your call, transcribes it on your own machine and shows one short, specific suggestion at a time: an objection handler, a buying signal to lean into, a question to ask. When the call ends you get a summary and a recommended next step.

Download for Windows View pricing

Also on Mac (Apple Silicon, beta): Download · Install guide

The problem

You cannot take notes, read a script and listen closely, all at once.The best moment to handle an objection is the moment it is raised. Raptor keeps the next move in front of you while you stay in the conversation.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Start a call session

Pick the prospect so the coaching has context: their company, their pain points and the patterns that have won for you before.

02

Transcribe locally

Speech is turned into text on your own computer with a local Whisper-based model. Your audio is not uploaded to a transcription service.

03

Get one suggestion at a time

Every few seconds Raptor reads what was just said and offers at most two sentences: how to answer a price objection, when to ask for next steps, what to ask next.

04

Spot objections and buying signals

Phrases like "how much" or "next steps" are recognised as buying signals; phrases like "send me more information" are matched to a proven response.

05

Capture the outcome

After the call: sentiment, the objections raised, a short summary and one recommended next action, saved to the record.

What you get

Built in.

Real-time coaching

Short, specific and actionable, streamed into the app while the call is live.

Built-in objection handling

Eight common objections (price, timing, needing to think, an incumbent solution and more), each mapped to a reframe.

Post-call intelligence

Sentiment, objections, a two to three sentence summary and a next step for every call.

Feeds the learning loop

Every call is recorded as an interaction so the engine learns what converts.

You are responsible for telling people when a call is being transcribed and for following the call-recording laws that apply to you. See our Terms.
