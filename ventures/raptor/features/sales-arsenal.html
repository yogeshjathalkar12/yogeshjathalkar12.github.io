Sales Arsenal

Twenty-one tools you would otherwise pay for separately: nine free calculators that run in your browser with no sign-up, plus twelve working tools inside the Raptor app.

Download for Windows View pricing
The problem

Sales teams end up with a drawer full of single-purpose subscriptions.Market sizing in one place, email verification in another, timezone scheduling in a third. The Arsenal puts them together.

Every calculation runs in your browser.

Free calculators (no sign-up)

TAM / SAM / SOM Calculator

Size your market and get a realistic three-year capture projection.

Sales Velocity Calculator

Find the revenue lever that moves the most.

ICP Profile Builder

Generate your ideal client profile document.

Pipeline Health Score

Diagnose your pipeline before it breaks.

Commission Builder

Design a sales comp plan with projections.

Outreach Timing Optimizer

Work out when and how to reach a prospect.

Churn Risk Diagnostic

Score accounts before they walk out.

Sales Hiring ROI Calculator

SDR or AE? See the numbers first.

Sequence Planner

Build the outreach sequence, not just the email.

Available after you sign in to the Raptor web app.

In-app tools

Infrastructure Diagnostic Suite

Deliverability checks: blacklists, DNS records and hop-by-hop mail routing.

Inbox Sync

Tracks cold-outreach threads by email headers and filters out-of-office replies.

Email Variant Generator and Campaign Queue

Distinct variations of each email, queued for sending, to protect sender reputation.

Website Visitor ID

Matches visitor IP addresses to company networks to spot accounts on your site.

Smart Timezone Scheduler

Turns a location into the right local send time, including daylight-saving changes.

Call Audio Cleaner and VAD

Strips silence and noise from call recordings in your browser.

Smart ICP Profiler

Clusters customers on revenue, headcount and engagement to refine your ICP.

Video Payload Compressor

Compresses sales videos and strips hidden metadata.

Real-Time Email Verifier

Checks DNS and does a server handshake to verify an address without sending a test mail.

Email Open and Engagement Tracker

An invisible tracking pixel that shows when a message is opened.

AI Content Suite (bring your own key)

Draft emails and campaign copy with your own OpenAI, Google or Anthropic key, with no markup.

Revenue and Pipeline Simulator

Statistical simulations on your pipeline for realistic revenue ranges.
