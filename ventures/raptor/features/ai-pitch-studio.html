AI Pitch Studio

Raptor writes each pitch for one specific person at one specific company, using your Company DNA, that prospect's dossier and the sales angle your own results say works best. Then it fact-checks the draft against your DNA before you see it.

Download for Windows View pricing

Also on Mac (Apple Silicon, beta): Download · Install guide

The problem

Templates get ignored, and unchecked AI makes things up.Mail-merge pitches get deleted. Raw AI-written ones can invent features, customers or numbers you do not have, which is worse than a template. Raptor personalises and verifies.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Pick the angle

The learning engine chooses the pitch angle that has performed best for your industry so far, with a little deliberate exploration so it keeps learning.

02

Draft from real facts

A local model writes a subject and body using the facts in your DNA and the prospect's dossier, for email or WhatsApp.

03

Fact-check the draft

A separate, non-generative validation model compares each claim in the draft against your DNA and flags anything unsupported.

04

Self-correct, or fall back safely

Flagged drafts go back for a rewrite with the specific problems listed. If it still cannot pass, Raptor uses a safe, deterministic pitch instead of sending something risky.

What you get

Built in.

Personalised email and WhatsApp copy

A subject and body written for the person, not merged into a template.

Hallucination guardrail

Unsupported claims are caught before they reach a prospect, not after.

Angle tracking

Every pitch records which angle it used, so results flow straight back into learning.

Injection-resistant

Text scraped from a prospect's site is treated as untrusted and cannot instruct the model.

The validator reduces made-up claims but cannot eliminate them. Read a pitch before you send it, as you would with any draft.
