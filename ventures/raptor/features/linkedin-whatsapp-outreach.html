LinkedIn & WhatsApp Outreach

Reach people where email is not enough. On LinkedIn, Raptor writes the connection note and you send it yourself, so your account is never put at risk. On WhatsApp, run broadcasts, flows and a shared inbox on your own Meta number, or send a one-to-one message from a prepared link.

The problem

Most prospects are not reachable by email alone.Raptor prepares the right message on the right channel and leaves the one click that matters to you.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Try the next channel

When no email can be found for a prospect, Raptor looks for a decision maker's LinkedIn profile. WhatsApp is a last resort, only if the company publishes a number itself and you have switched it on.

02

Raptor writes, you send

On LinkedIn you press Copy note and open profile, paste the note and send it. On WhatsApp the message opens with the text filled in and you press send. Then you tell Raptor you sent it.

03

Your accounts stay safe

Raptor never logs in to LinkedIn or automates it, because scripted logins break LinkedIn's terms and are a common way accounts get restricted.

04

Know when they answer

Raptor notices LinkedIn's own emails when someone accepts or messages you, and an email reply from the same person. For numbers on the WhatsApp Business API, replies arrive through a secure relay and are verified on your computer. Otherwise a button tells Raptor.

05

Learn which channel works

Every send is recorded against its channel, so the learning loop can compare LinkedIn and WhatsApp with email on your own results.

What you get

Built in.

Assisted LinkedIn notes

A short, personal connection note per prospect, under LinkedIn's length limit, written from your Company DNA.

WhatsApp from published numbers

Off by default. Only numbers the company publishes itself are used, and never bought or guessed.

WhatsApp Business API inbox

For your own Meta number: replies land in the Replies tab with a suggested answer, and a STOP is honoured permanently.

Full WhatsApp suite

Broadcasts, drip sequences, keyword triggers, AI flows and a shared inbox, in the desktop app and the web app.

Automation does not make outreach lawful. Messaging people who have not asked to hear from you can break local marketing rules, and WhatsApp's own policies apply to your number. You decide who to contact and you are responsible for consent and opt-outs. See our Terms.
