Autonomous Prospecting

Raptor turns your Company DNA into search queries, finds companies that look like your best customers, researches each one and ranks them, with the reasons shown, so your team spends its time on the accounts most likely to buy.

Download for Windows View pricing
The problem

Prospecting is research, and research does not scale.Reps burn hours reading websites and news to decide who is worth a message. Most of it is repeatable: check the tech stack, scan for growth signals, find the right person. Raptor does the repeatable part.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Generate targeted searches

Your DNA becomes a set of search queries covering your target industries, tech stack and pain points.

02

Collect candidates

Results come from web search and free public company databases. You can also add prospects manually or in bulk.

03

Enrich each company

Raptor reads the company's site and recent news, detects its technology stack, and looks for likely decision-makers and contact patterns.

04

Detect buying triggers

Funding, hiring, new leadership, product launches, stated pain and growth signals are flagged along with the evidence behind them.

05

Score and rank

Every prospect gets a 0 to 100 fit score with plain-language reasons, and lands in a tier: Elite, Strong, Moderate or Poor.

What you get

Built in.

Explainable fit score

Scoring is deterministic: tech-stack match, pain-keyword match and buying signals. Each score lists exactly why it came out as it did. No black box.

Prospect dossiers

One dossier per company: what they do, their technology, recent news, triggers and contacts, ready to read before you reach out.

Timing hints

Trigger-based suggestions for when to reach out, so a message lands soon after something changes at the company.

One step to pipeline

Promote any prospect straight into your pipeline and start the outreach journey.
