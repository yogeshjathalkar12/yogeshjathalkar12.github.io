Feature 03 · Intelligence

Competitive Intelligence

Raptor finds the companies you are up against, reads their sites, and writes a structured battle card for each. Then it quietly watches for changes, so the card does not go stale.

Download for Windows View pricing

Also on Mac (Apple Silicon, beta): Download · Install guide

The problem

Battle cards go out of date the day after someone writes them.Competitors change pricing and positioning constantly, and nobody has time to re-read ten websites every month. Raptor keeps watch for you.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Discover competitors

From your Company DNA, Raptor identifies the companies most likely to compete with you. You can add your own too.

02

Read each competitor

A local model analyses each competitor's site, and the result is checked for a valid structure before it is saved.

03

Build the battle card

Each card records their pricing model, how they position themselves, their strengths and weaknesses, and how to beat them.

04

Watch for changes

A background radar checks competitor pages for meaningful changes, ignoring cosmetic style and script noise, and re-runs the analysis when something real changes.

05

Use it

Cards are searchable on your machine and available in your Raptor workspace on the web.

What you get

Built in.

Structured battle cards

Pricing model, positioning, strengths, weaknesses and how to beat them, in one consistent format for every competitor.

Continuous monitoring

Change detection that triggers a fresh analysis only when the page has genuinely changed.

Polite by design

Raptor checks robots.txt before it fetches a page and blocks unsafe internal addresses.

Synced to your workspace

Battle cards can sync to the web app so the whole team can read them.
