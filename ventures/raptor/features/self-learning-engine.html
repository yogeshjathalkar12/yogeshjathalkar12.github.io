Self-Learning Engine

Raptor records what happens after every pitch, reply, meeting, call and deal, learns which angles, channels and timings win for your business, and applies that to the next prospect. It is a measured feedback loop, and you can see the numbers.

Download for Windows View pricing

Also on Mac (Apple Silicon, beta): Download · Install guide

The problem

Most tools forget everything.A CRM stores outcomes, but it does not change what you do next. Raptor closes the loop.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Record every interaction

Pitches sent, replies, meetings, calls and deals are logged on your machine first, so it works offline and nothing is lost.

02

Analyse what is working

Outcomes are grouped by angle, channel, industry and day of week to show which combinations convert.

03

Choose the next move

For each new prospect Raptor picks an angle, channel and timing: mostly the proven winner, with about one choice in ten deliberately exploring alternatives so it keeps finding better options.

04

Sharpen the fit model

Similarity to your best customers, measured with sentence embeddings, refines who counts as an ideal prospect as wins accumulate.

05

Report upward

A strategic report summarises what the data says: what is winning, what is not, and where to focus.

What you get

Built in.

Pattern dashboard

See angle performance by outcome instead of guessing what is working.

Angle, channel and timing selection

Recommendations that are recomputed as new results arrive.

Strategic report

A leadership-style summary of what the learning data shows.

Offline-first

Interactions are written locally first and synced afterwards, so a dropped connection loses nothing.

Honest expectations: with only a handful of interactions there is very little to learn from. The engine improves with volume, so expect it to get sharper over weeks of use, not on day one.

Under the hood
Method
Epsilon-greedy multi-armed bandit, 10% exploration
Fit model
Sentence embeddings and cosine similarity
Stored
Locally first, then synced to your workspace
