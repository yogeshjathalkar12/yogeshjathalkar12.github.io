Free CRM

A complete sales CRM comes with Raptor: contacts, deals, pipeline, tickets, activity and analytics, connected to everything above, so what Raptor learns shows up where your team already works. It is free to start.

The problem

Most CRMs are a place to type things in.Raptor's CRM is the place where discovery, pitching, calls and outreach land, so your pipeline reflects what actually happened without retyping it.

Step by step.
