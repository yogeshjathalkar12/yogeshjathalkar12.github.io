Feature 01 · Foundation

Company DNA

Every other feature runs on this one. Raptor reads your website and your own documents, then builds a structured profile of what you sell, who you sell to and how your team talks, so every prospect, pitch and call suggestion is grounded in your real business instead of a generic template.

Download for Windows View pricing

Also on Mac (Apple Silicon, beta): Download · Install guide

The problem

A general-purpose AI has never seen your pricing, your case studies or the objections your team hears every week.Ask it for a pitch and you get plausible-sounding filler. Raptor starts by learning you.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Point it at your website

Raptor crawls your site, converts pages to clean text and prioritises the pages that describe what you actually do (products, customers, pricing) over boilerplate.

02

Add your own material

Upload PDFs, spreadsheets, CSVs and text files: decks, brochures, sales scripts, objection sheets. Raptor extracts the text and folds it into the profile.

03

A local model extracts your DNA

A language model running on your own computer pulls out structured facts: products, team, sales scripts, objection handlers, target industries, tech stack and pain keywords.

04

Review, score and refine

You get a DNA completeness score and a printable report. Add sources or reprocess any time; the profile is yours to correct.

What you get

Built in.

Structured company profile

Products, value proposition, target industries, target tech stack and pain keywords, in a form the rest of Raptor uses directly.

DNA score

A completeness score that shows what is missing, so you know whether the foundation is strong enough to hunt on.

Downloadable DNA report

A PDF summary of what Raptor has learned about your company. Useful for onboarding new reps, too.

Keyword graph and sources

See the themes Raptor found and which pages or documents each fact came from.
