Why choose Raptor

Not a sales tool.
A sales brain.

Most sales software is a set of specialists that do not talk to each other and never learn. Raptor is one system that owns the whole loop, keeps your data on your machine, and gets better at your business the longer you use it.

Six reasons

What makes it different.

01

One loop, not five tools

Discovery, pitching, calling and follow-up share one memory. What Raptor learns on a call changes the next pitch; what a pitch earns changes the next search. Nothing needs copying between apps.

See all features →
02

It learns what works for you

Every reply, meeting and deal is recorded and used to choose the next angle, channel and timing. It is a measured feedback loop, so you can see the numbers behind every recommendation.

How the learning works →
03

Checked before it is sent

A separate validation model compares each claim in a draft against your Company DNA and sends flagged drafts back for a rewrite. AI that invents features is a liability, so we built the check in.

How pitches are checked →
04

Your data, on your machine

Your Company DNA, prospects and pitches live on your computer, and the AI runs locally, so your prospect data is not sent to a third-party AI provider and there are no per-call AI fees.

05

You can see why

Fit scores are transparent: technology match, pain-point match and buying signals, on a 0 to 100 scale, with the reasons listed for every prospect. No black-box ranking to trust on faith.

06

Start free, with a CRM and 21 tools

A free CRM, email and WhatsApp modules, nine free calculators and twelve in-app tools come with Raptor, so you are not paying for five subscriptions before you have proof.

The honest part

Where Raptor may not fit yet.

Windows first

The desktop engine is Windows x64 today. The web app (CRM, email, WhatsApp and the tools) runs in any modern browser.

Local AI needs a capable PC

Running models on your own machine means Raptor works best on a reasonably modern computer. Older laptops will feel slower.

Public-source prospecting

Raptor discovers and enriches companies from public web sources and your own lists. It does not ship a licensed database of millions of pre-verified contacts.

It learns as you use it

With few interactions there is little to learn from. Expect the engine to get sharper over weeks of real use, not on day one.

We would rather you know this before you download than after.
