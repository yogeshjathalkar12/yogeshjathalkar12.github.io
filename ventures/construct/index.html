Sentinel Safety

Reduce liability by 100%. Our AI detects safety violations instantly, preventing costly accidents and slashing insurance premiums.

Sentinel Safety runs a 360-degree computer vision layer across every active zone on site, watching for missing PPE, unsafe proximity to machinery, unauthorized zone entry, and fall hazards in real time — no manual patrols required.

Every flagged incident is timestamped, geotagged, and pushed to site supervisors within seconds, so corrective action happens before an accident does, not after an insurance claim.
ID: CNSTRCT-02

Quantum Logistics

Zero material shrinkage. We track every brick and beam in real-time, ensuring you never pay for lost or stolen assets again.

Using a hybrid RFID and Bluetooth Low Energy mesh, Quantum Logistics tags material and equipment the moment it enters the gate, then follows it through storage, transfer, and installation across the entire footprint of the site.

Discrepancies between what was purchased and what was actually used surface automatically, closing the gap where theft, waste, and paperwork errors usually hide.
ID: CNSTRCT-03

Predictive Oracle

Eliminate budget overruns. Our AI predicts financial bottlenecks weeks in advance, protecting your profit margin from delays.

Predictive Oracle runs Monte Carlo simulations against live procurement, labor, and weather data, modeling thousands of possible cost trajectories for your project every single day.

Instead of discovering a budget overrun in a quarterly review, you see it forming twelve weeks out — with enough runway to renegotiate, reschedule, or reroute before it hits the bottom line.
ID: CNSTRCT-04

Spectra Quality

Zero structural rework. We detect micro-fractures during the pour, ensuring perfect structural integrity and avoiding million-dollar repairs.

Thermal and LiDAR sensors scan every concrete pour and structural weld as it happens, cross-referencing the readings against engineering tolerances to catch defects invisible to the naked eye.

Anomalies generate an auto-compiled PDF report with exact coordinates, so remediation happens while the crew and equipment are still on-site — not months later when it's ten times the cost.
ID: CNSTRCT-05

Eco Recovery

Turn waste into revenue. We automate recycling tracking to meet Green Standards and monetize scrap material streams.

Eco Recovery logs every load of waste and scrap leaving the site, sorting it against LEED and BREEAM diversion categories automatically instead of relying on manual paper logs at the dumpster.

The result is audit-ready compliance documentation on demand, plus visibility into which scrap streams are worth selling instead of hauling away at a loss.
ID: CNSTRCT-06

Bio-Ergonomics

Maximize workforce uptime. We prevent heatstroke and fatigue, ensuring your team operates at peak efficiency safely.

Wearable sensors track heart rate, core temperature trends, and wet-bulb globe temperature across the site, flagging individual workers approaching heat-stress thresholds before symptoms appear.

All health data stays GDPR-compliant and worker-anonymized at the dashboard level, so you get safety without turning it into a surveillance and trust problem.
ID: CNSTRCT-07

Digital Twin

Perfect reconciliation. Compare "As-Built" vs "As-Designed" daily to catch deviation errors in millimeters, not meters.

Digital Twin maintains a live 4D model of the site — three spatial dimensions plus construction sequencing over time — synced continuously against IFC and Revit source files.

When actual construction drifts from design intent, the deviation is visualized instantly instead of surfacing during a punch-list walkthrough weeks later.
ID: CNSTRCT-08

Reality Capture

Automated daily surveying. Our drones generate point clouds in minutes, giving you a daily snapshot of progress.

Autonomous drone flights use LiDAR SLAM to capture high-resolution point clouds of the entire site every day, processed at the edge so results are ready before the morning safety briefing.

Progress tracking, volumetric earthwork calculations, and stakeholder reporting all pull from the same daily capture — no separate survey crew required.
ID: CNSTRCT-09

Vortex Neural Engine

Automated site management. The central brain that coordinates sensors and data, making decisions while you sleep.

Vortex is the coordination layer that every other Construct module reports into — safety alerts, logistics data, financial forecasts, and quality scans converge here over an encrypted mesh network with 99.99% uptime.

It doesn't just store the data; it acts on it, routing alerts to the right person and triggering workflows automatically, so the site keeps running even during off-hours.
ID: CNSTRCT-10

Construct Rover

24/7 autonomous logistics. Our heavy-lift rovers move material day and night, keeping your site moving non-stop.

Each rover carries up to 500kg and operates with Level 4 autonomy, navigating site terrain, avoiding personnel and obstacles, and running continuously for up to 12 hours per charge cycle.

Material moves happen on the schedule the project needs, not on the availability of a driver's shift — cutting idle time between deliveries to near zero.
