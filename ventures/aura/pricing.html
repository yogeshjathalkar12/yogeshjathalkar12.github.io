How credits work

Logging symptoms, uploading documents, tracking medications and inviting delegates are always free: that is your record, and it should never be metered. A credit is spent only when you generate a share link that hands a scoped view of your vault to someone outside it, like a doctor. That way Vapus earns from the act of sharing, never from the act of remembering.

Do credits expire?

The terms for credits are in our Terms & Conditions.

Is a credit refunded if a link fails?

If a link cannot be created after a credit is taken, the credit is returned automatically. A link you revoke yourself is not refunded.

How do I get more credits?

Get in touch through the contact page and we will help.
