Intelligence · Nutrition

Log a meal with a photoIn development

Estimate calories and nutrients from a picture of your food, and keep them beside your symptoms.

Create your vaultAll features

What it is designed to do

  • Recognise common foods from a photo using computer vision.
  • Estimate calories and key nutrients per meal.
  • Log meals to your timeline, so you can see if a food and a symptom go together.
  • Let you correct any guess before it is saved.

How it fits into your vault

Meals would sit on your timeline next to symptoms and medications, so patterns across food, medicine and how you feel are visible in one place.

Status

In development. This module is not available in the Vapus app yet. We will announce it here when it is.

What it is not

Estimates from a photo are rough and will be wrong, especially for mixed dishes and portions. It is not dietary advice, and must not be used to make medication decisions, for example insulin dosing. Vapus's insights are informational. They are not a diagnosis and are not a substitute for a clinician's judgement.
