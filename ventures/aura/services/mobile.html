What it is designed to do

  • Log a symptom in a few taps, the moment it happens.
  • Let family members you trust see trends, within the access level you gave them.
  • Send alerts you have chosen to receive.
  • Use the same vault and permissions as the web app.

How it fits into your vault

It is the same vault as the web app. Nothing is copied elsewhere, and the same permission levels apply.

Status

The Aura web app already works in a phone browser and adapts to small screens. A dedicated native app is planned but not available yet.

What it is not

Alerts depend on your phone and connection, and can be delayed or missed. Do not rely on them in an emergency. Aura's insights are informational. They are not a diagnosis and are not a substitute for a clinician's judgement.
