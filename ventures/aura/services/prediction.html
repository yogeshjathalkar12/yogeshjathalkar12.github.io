What it is designed to do

  • Look across your timeline, medications and any readings for changes that are worth a second look.
  • Learn your own baseline instead of comparing you with population averages.
  • Suggest when it may be worth logging more, or speaking to a doctor.
  • Let you dismiss any suggestion, so you stay in control.

How it fits into your vault

Suggestions would appear beside the entries they relate to on your timeline. Nothing is shared with anyone unless you share it.

Status

In development. This module is not available in the Aura app yet. We will announce it here when it is.

What it is not

It is not a diagnosis, and it can be wrong. Predictions built from a limited personal history are uncertain, and a missing warning does not mean you are well. Aura's insights are informational. They are not a diagnosis and are not a substitute for a clinician's judgement.
