Intelligence · Environment

What is in the air, next to how you feelIn development

Air quality, pollution and hazard information, set alongside your symptom timeline so triggers are easier to spot.

Create your vaultAll features

What it is designed to do

  • Show local air quality (AQI) and pollution for the places you choose.
  • Line environmental conditions up with your timeline, so you can see if symptoms follow poor-air days.
  • Surface notices about local hazards.
  • Use your location only if you choose to allow it.

How it fits into your vault

Environmental conditions would appear as context on your timeline. They would be additional information, never a change to what you logged.

Status

In development. This module is not available in the Vapus app yet. We will announce it here when it is.

What it is not

Two things happening together does not mean one caused the other. It does not replace public health or emergency warnings from your local authorities. Vapus's insights are informational. They are not a diagnosis and are not a substitute for a clinician's judgement.
