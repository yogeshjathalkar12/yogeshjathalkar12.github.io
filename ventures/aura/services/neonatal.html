Intelligence · Neonatal

Gentler monitoring for the smallest patientsIn development

Non-invasive monitoring designed around thermal and optical sensing, so a baby is observed without wires or adhesives.

Create your vaultAll features

What it is designed to do

  • Observe a baby without wires or adhesive sensors.
  • Combine thermal and optical signals to estimate basic vital signs.
  • Stream observations into the family vault.
  • Notify the trusted people you choose.

How it fits into your vault

Readings would be written to the family vault, and the people you have given access to could see them.

Status

Research and early prototyping. It is not available, and it is not cleared for clinical use.

What it is not

It is not a medical device, it has not been clinically validated, and it must never replace supervision or clinical monitoring of a baby. Any system used in a hospital would need clinical validation and the required regulatory approvals first. Vapus's insights are informational. They are not a diagnosis and are not a substitute for a clinician's judgement.
