Intelligence · Trainer

Feedback on your form, from your cameraIn development

Camera-based pose estimation to count reps and point out form problems, logged as part of your activity history.

Create your vaultAll features

What it is designed to do

  • Count repetitions automatically.
  • Point out common form problems as you move.
  • Log workouts to your activity history.
  • Adapt to what you are working on over time.

How it fits into your vault

Workouts would be part of your record, so activity sits beside symptoms and medication.

Status

In development. This module is not available in the Aura app yet. We will announce it here when it is. Before launch we will publish exactly how video from your camera is handled.

What it is not

It is not a physiotherapist or a doctor. Stop if you feel pain, and speak to a professional before starting a programme if you have a health condition. Aura's insights are informational. They are not a diagnosis and are not a substitute for a clinician's judgement.
