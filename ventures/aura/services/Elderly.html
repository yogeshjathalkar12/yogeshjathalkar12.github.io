Intelligence · Elderly

Quiet watchfulness, with the right people toldIn development

Dedicated monitoring ideas for sleep, falls and heart rhythm, with alerts routed to the people you trust.

Create your vaultAll features

What it is designed to do

  • Watch for signs of disturbed breathing during sleep.
  • Detect falls.
  • Notice irregular heart rhythms.
  • Route alerts to the delegates you have chosen, not to everyone.

How it fits into your vault

Alerts would go to your existing delegates, at the access level you gave them. Events would be recorded on the timeline.

Status

Concept and early research. It is not available.

What it is not

False alarms and missed events are both possible. It is not a medical alert service, and it must not be your only safeguard for someone who is at risk. In an emergency, call your local emergency number. Aura's insights are informational. They are not a diagnosis and are not a substitute for a clinician's judgement.
