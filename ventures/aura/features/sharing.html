Your Vault · Sharing

Show your doctor exactly what mattersAvailable in the app

A share link opens a read-only view of the parts of your record you choose. Your doctor needs no account, and the link stops working when it expires.

Create your vaultOpen my vault

What it does

  • Choose what to include: timeline, medications, document list.
  • Set the expiry: 24 hours, 48 hours, 3 days or 7 days.
  • Label it (for example "Dr. Mehta, Cardiologist") so you remember who has it.
  • Doctors open it with no account and no app.
  • Revoke a link early and it stops working immediately.
  • See every link you have created.

How to use it

  1. Open ShareChoose Share in the sidebar.
  2. Pick the contents and expiryInclude only what your doctor needs.
  3. Generate the linkIt costs 1 share credit. The full link is shown once, so copy it then.
  4. Send it yourselfSend it to the intended person. Revoke it any time.

Anyone with the link can view it

Until it expires or you revoke it, anyone who has the link can open it. Send it only to the person you mean, ideally over a private channel.

Credits

Building your record is always free. A share credit is used only when you create a link. See Pricing.
