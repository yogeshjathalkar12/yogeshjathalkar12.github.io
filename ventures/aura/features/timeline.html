Your Vault · Timeline

A running story of how you feelAvailable in the app

Write down what you are experiencing, in your own words. Aura keeps it in order and groups related entries, so you never have to rely on memory in the doctor's room.

Create your vaultOpen my vault

What it does

  • Log a symptom in plain words, with an optional severity from 1 to 10 and up to 20 tags.
  • Entries made within 15 days of each other are grouped into one episode automatically.
  • Newest entries first. Delete any entry you no longer want.
  • Family helpers you invite can add entries for you (Log-only access) without being able to read your history.
  • Everything stays private to your account until you deliberately share it.

Why it helps

Doctors ask when it started and how bad it was. A timeline answers both without guesswork, and shows whether something is a one-off or a pattern.

How to use it

  1. Open TimelineSign in and choose Timeline in the sidebar.
  2. Write what is happeningOne or two sentences is enough. Add a severity and tags if you like.
  3. Come back as it changesNew entries join the same episode if they are within 15 days.
  4. Share when you need toTurn any part of your record into a read-only link for your doctor.

Good to know

Severity is your own 1 to 10 rating. Aura does not interpret entries, score them or diagnose anything.
