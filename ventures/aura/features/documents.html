Your Vault · Documents

One cabinet, not a dozen foldersAvailable in the app

Upload prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries and scans. They live in private storage that only your account can open.

Create your vaultOpen my vault

What it does

  • Upload PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP files up to 20 MB each.
  • Label each file: Prescription, Lab report, Discharge summary, Scan or Other, plus a short note.
  • Files are stored privately. Nobody can open one without being signed in with permission.
  • Opening a document uses a link that expires after 60 seconds, so a copied link stops working almost at once.
  • Deleting a document removes the record and the stored file.

How to use it

  1. Open DocumentsChoose Documents in the sidebar.
  2. Pick a file and a typeAdd a note such as "Blood test, March" so it is easy to find later.
  3. Save to vaultThe file is uploaded and added to your cabinet.
  4. Open or delete anytimeUse Open to view a file, or Delete to remove it permanently.

When you share with a doctor

A share link shows the list of your documents (type, note and date). The files themselves are not included in the shared view.

Keep your originals

Vapus is a convenient copy, not an archive service. Keep the original paper or digital record wherever your clinic issued it.
