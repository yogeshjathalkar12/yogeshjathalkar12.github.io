Your Vault · Family Access

Help from people you trustAvailable in the app

Invite someone with a personal access key. You pick what they can do, the key expires on its own, and you can remove their access at any moment.

Create your vaultOpen my vault

Three levels of access

✎

Log only

Can add timeline entries, documents and medicines for you. Cannot read anything back.

👁

Log & view

Everything in Log only, plus can read your timeline, medications and documents.

⚿

Full access

Everything above, plus can invite or remove other people, delete entries and create share links.

How it works

  1. Create an access keyEnter their email, choose a level and how long the key is valid (1, 3, 7 or 30 days).
  2. Send them the linkAura gives you a link to send. It does not email it for you.
  3. They sign in and acceptThey must sign in with the same email address you invited, and have confirmed it.
  4. Remove access whenever you likeOne click ends it immediately.

Built-in safeguards

An invite works once, only for the invited email, and only until it expires. Nobody can accept an invite to their own vault.

Coming soon

The server already enforces these permissions. A dedicated screen for switching into a vault someone has shared with you is still being built.
