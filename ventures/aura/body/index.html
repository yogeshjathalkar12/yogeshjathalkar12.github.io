    How to explore

    • Rotate: drag. Zoom: scroll or pinch. The zoom goes toward whatever is under your cursor, and more detail loads as you get closer.
    • Pan: right-drag, or two-finger drag. Reset view returns to the whole body.
    • Layers: switch body systems on and off, or drag Depth to peel from outer layers to deep ones. Peel layers as you zoom in does it for you.
    • Click a structure to name it, zoom to it, or hide it. Search to fly straight to any of the named structures.
    • On a phone, use the layer toggles to keep it smooth. Full detail is heavy, so it only loads when you zoom in.

    Some parts of the original Z-Anatomy model set are non-commercial or have no stated licence (for example the kidney model, the inner ear and the cortical brain surfaces), so they are not included here. That is why a few structures, such as the kidneys and the surface of the brain, are missing.
