About research

Vapus does not use or share anyone's health record for research today. If we ever offer an aggregated, anonymised research programme, it will be strictly opt-in, and we will explain it here before asking anyone to join.

For families and caregivers

If you look after a parent, a partner or a child, Family Access lets you help without sharing a password. You are responsible for looking after someone else's information lawfully and with their consent.
