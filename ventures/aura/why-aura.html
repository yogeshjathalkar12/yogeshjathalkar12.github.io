Why Aura

Your health has a story. It deserves a home.

Most of us keep our health history in five places at once: a folder of PDFs, photos in a chat, a doctor's portal, and memory. Aura puts it in one vault that only you control.

Create your vaultHow it works

The problem

  • You repeat your history to every new doctor, and forget half of it.
  • Reports live in email attachments, chat threads and photo galleries.
  • "When did this start?" and "Did you take it today?" are hard to answer from memory.
  • Sharing a record with a doctor usually means forwarding files that then stay in an inbox forever.

What Aura does differently

⚿

You own the record

You decide who sees what, and for how long. Nothing is shared by default.

◷

Free to keep, pay only to share

Logging, uploading and tracking are free. A credit is used only when you create a doctor share link.

✓

Honest about its limits

Aura is a record and a set of tools. It is not a doctor, and it says so wherever it matters.

What we believe

  • Your health data belongs to you.
  • Sharing should be a deliberate act, with an end date.
  • Software for health should say clearly what it can and cannot do.
  • The record should get more useful the longer you use it.
