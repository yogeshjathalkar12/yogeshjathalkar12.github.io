Trust, By Design

Nothing leaves the vault without you.

Aura is built on the same principle every time: the vault owner decides who sees what, and for how long.

🔒

Encrypted end to end

Data is encrypted both at rest and in transit, so a record in motion is as protected as one sitting in storage.

🧩

Row-level access control

Every record is scoped at the database level to its owner — not just hidden in the interface, but unreachable without permission.

⚿

Granular delegate permissions

Log-only, log & view, or full access — you choose exactly how much a caregiver can see or do, and revoke it anytime.

↗

Time-boxed, credit-gated sharing

Doctor share links expire on their own and require a deliberate action to create — nothing is shared by default.

🛰️

Isolated by design

Aura runs on its own dedicated infrastructure, kept separate from every other Shoonya Origins venture — no shared data, no pooling.

🗂️

Anonymised by default for research

Any data used for aggregate insight is stripped of identity first, and only with your explicit opt-in.
