Conversational Onboarding

A blank profile form is intimidating, especially for a student who's never had career guidance before. Eklavya lets you just talk instead — and pulls the structured details out for you.

Two ways to build your profile

Chat naturally

Tell Eklavya's assistant about yourself in your own words — your age, what you're studying, what you enjoy, what you're aiming for. It extracts the structured fields as you go.

Or fill a structured form

Prefer forms? A traditional multi-step form captures the exact same fields directly, no conversation required. Use either, or both — they write to the same profile.

What that actually looks like

I'm in class 11, I love biology and want to become a doctor.
Noted: 1 academic record, 1 interest (Medicine & Healthcare), profile basics. Anything else you'd like to add?

Every extraction is checked before it's trusted

What the assistant pulls out of a conversation is validated against a strict schema before anything is saved — malformed or unclear extractions are never silently stored as fact. And your conversation is treated as data to extract from, never as instructions the assistant should follow, which protects the extraction process from being manipulated.

Data handling: raw conversation transcripts are kept only for a limited period after which they're automatically deleted — see our Privacy Policy for details. Only the structured facts extracted from a conversation remain in your profile afterward, clearly tagged as AI-extracted rather than manually entered.

Try it yourself

Chat or fill a form — whichever feels easier.
