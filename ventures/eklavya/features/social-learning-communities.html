Social Learning Communities

Career guidance doesn't have to be solitary. Eklavya connects you with peers pursuing similar paths — for motivation, resource-sharing, and a sense that you're not figuring this out alone.

How groups form

As you declare interests during onboarding, Eklavya automatically places you into the matching interest-based groups once your profile is reasonably complete — no manual searching for "the right community" required. You can also browse and join additional groups yourself.

Interest-based groups

Communities organized around specific interests — from data science to fine arts — so conversations stay relevant to what you actually care about.

A personalized feed

Posts from your groups, achievements shared by peers, and platform updates, ranked so the most relevant content surfaces first.

Peer milestone sharing

Share wins — a strong exam score, a certification, a competition placement — and see how peers on a similar path are progressing.

Likes and engagement

Simple engagement signals (likes) help surface what the community finds genuinely useful, feeding back into how the feed is ranked.

How your feed is ranked

Relevance = Interest Match × 0.4 + Recency × 0.3 + Engagement × 0.3

Content from groups you've actually joined is weighted more heavily than general posts, recent posts are favored over old ones, and posts with real engagement rise — so your feed stays useful even as the community grows.

Find your community

Declare your interests and Eklavya connects you with peers on the same path.
