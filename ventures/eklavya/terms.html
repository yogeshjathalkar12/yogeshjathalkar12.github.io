2. What Eklavya is

Eklavya is a longitudinal career-guidance platform: it helps you build an evolving profile of your academic history, interests, skills, and achievements, and uses that profile to surface scholarships, colleges, internships, certifications, and job opportunities that may be relevant to you. Where Eklavya is deployed alongside a school's learning platform, your course activity on that platform may automatically contribute signal to your Eklavya profile — this is described in detail in our Privacy Policy.
