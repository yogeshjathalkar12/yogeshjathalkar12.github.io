01 The Problem

The tools exist.
The knowledge exists.
So why is research
still this hard?

// Fragmented
Critical tools are scattered across proprietary software and expensive licences. A grad student in Pune shouldn't need MATLAB just to run a basic quantum calculation.
// Siloed
Researchers work in isolation. Datasets, methods, and insights get buried in lab servers and unread appendices. The same problems get solved over and over, separately, by people who never meet.
// Inaccessible
The infrastructure of science concentrates in elite institutions. The next great discovery might be stuck in a university with no Mathematica licence and no research network. We fix that.
