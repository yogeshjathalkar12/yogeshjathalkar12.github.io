Core technology

The foundations behind the software.

Everything we build rests on a few areas of computer science that we work on ourselves, not just use. We bring that core technology to software.

01

Local & native AI

We create AI that runs on your own computers and devices instead of depending on someone else’s servers: local, built to work natively, and under your control.

02

More calculation, from the same computer

Research into how a computer can carry out many more calculations: better algorithms, better use of the hardware, and smarter ways of organising the work.

03

Computer vision

Developing computer vision so that computers understand the world they look at in a better way, and can make sense of images and video the way people do.

04

Understanding human language

Helping computers understand human languages and the humans who use them, so that people can work with machines in their own words.

05

Sustainable AI

Working towards the sustainable development of AI: systems that can keep growing and getting better without wasting energy and resources.

06

Algorithms & systems

We create new algorithms to solve complex problems, and we study how computing systems really work, because good solutions come from understanding the whole system.

07

AI, data science & big data

We develop AI, data science and big data work end to end: collecting, storing and understanding very large amounts of data, and turning it into answers people can use.

Research

The answers become algorithms, and the algorithms become software that you can use.
