Anonymous Reporting

Snap a photo of a civic problem, drop a pin, pick a category. That is the whole report. Basis does not ask for your name, phone number or an account for anonymous reports.

Report an issue All features
The problem

A complaint phoned into a helpline gets logged and forgotten.Basis turns fifteen seconds of your time into a structured, geo-tagged record that can be counted, clustered and followed up.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Choose your city and the issue

Mumbai, Pune or another city, and one of eight categories: pothole or road damage, garbage or illegal dumping, sewage or drainage, broken infrastructure, traffic violation, littering, streetlight failure, or other.

02

Drop the pin

Use your live location, or drag the pin on the map to the exact spot. You can add a landmark in words if that helps.

03

Attach a photo

JPG, PNG, HEIC or WebP, up to 10 MB. The file type is checked by its actual contents, not just its extension.

04

Submit

The report is sent over an encrypted connection. To limit spam, a single browser can send a few reports in a short window.

What you get

The details.

Map-based location picker

An OpenStreetMap view with a draggable pin and a one-tap live location button.

Eight issue categories

Enough to route a report to the right kind of civic department, simple enough to pick in a second.

Abuse protection built in

Rate limiting, allow-listed inputs, file-signature checks and coordinate validation run before anything is sent.

Create an account only if you want rewards

Anonymous stays anonymous. An account is optional and only needed to earn city credits.

Photos can carry hidden metadata such as capture time or GPS position, depending on your phone's camera settings. Turn off location tagging in your camera if you do not want that included. See the Privacy Policy for details.
