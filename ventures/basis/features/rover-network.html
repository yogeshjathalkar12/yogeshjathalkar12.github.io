Rover Network

Citizen reports tell us where to look. Autonomous ground rovers are planned as the physical verification layer: patrolling, photographing and confirming what is actually on the street.

Get in touch All features
The problem

Reports depend on someone noticing, and on the photo being good.Rovers are meant to add consistent, repeatable ground truth, starting in the two cities where Alaka is active.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Phase 00: data collection

Citizen reports are already being received. This is the data layer that everything else builds on.

02

Phase 01: Mumbai and Pune

Planned physical patrols in Mumbai and Pune, with multi-sensor rigs, cameras and on-board computer vision.

03

Phase 02: more cities

Planned expansion to other major Indian cities once the approach is proven.

04

The same model, more data

Rover imagery would feed the same shared detection model that citizen photos improve.

What you get

The details.

Ground truth

Independent confirmation of what reports describe.

Consistent coverage

Systematic routes instead of whoever happens to notice.

Feeds the shared model

More varied imagery to train on.

Local first

Mumbai and Pune before anywhere else.
