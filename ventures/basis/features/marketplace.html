Marketplace

Spend the credits you have earned on everyday sustainable goods listed directly by local shops. No cash needed, just credits you already have.

Open dashboard All features
The problem

Good habits are easier to keep when they lead somewhere.The marketplace connects the credits citizens earn to real products from businesses in their own neighbourhood.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Browse

Open the marketplace panel in your dashboard to see products from registered local shops, each with a credit price.

02

Buy with credits

Choose a product and confirm. The credits are deducted from your wallet.

03

Get a redemption code

You receive a code for that purchase.

04

Collect from the shop

Show the code to the shop that listed the product.

What you get

The details.

Household

Everyday household goods.

Personal care

Personal care products.

Food & grocery

Food and grocery items.

Stationery

Stationery and more.

Products are listed and fulfilled by the shops themselves. Basis is the marketplace, not the seller. Stock, pickup and quality are the shop's responsibility.
