Free Tools

Thirty free calculators for the practical questions of city life, in three toolsets. They run entirely in your browser: no sign-up, no account, and what you type into them is not sent anywhere.

Open safety tools All features
The problem

Most people cannot tell whether an exit is wide enough, a rooftop can harvest useful rainwater, or a stamp-duty quote is right.These tools turn those questions into a two-minute calculation with a clear answer and, where it applies, the next step to take.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Safety and structure

Fire exit clearance, seismic risk, structural load, pothole risk, crowd density, acoustic comfort, air quality, flood risk, rooftop solar and infrastructure checks.

02

Environment and resources

Rainwater harvesting, per-person water use, greywater reuse, urban heat island, garden and compost planning, solar, biogas, tree offset, EV and carbon footprint.

03

Civic and everyday

Walkability audit, water tanker cost, streetlight, total cost of ownership, society maintenance, electricity bill, property tax, stamp duty, movers cost and an RTI request generator.

04

Use the result

Several tools end with a suggested action, such as which authority to write to or what to ask for.
