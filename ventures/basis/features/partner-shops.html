Partner Shops & Vouchers

Local shops and cafés can register as Basis partners, list products, issue their own discount vouchers and log verified waste disposals, reaching citizens who are already earning credits nearby.

Create account All features
The problem

Small local businesses rarely get a share of civic-tech attention.Basis gives them a way to reach engaged neighbours without ad spend, and to reward the customers who do the right thing.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Register your shop

Add your shop name, category, contact and address, and pin it on the map.

02

List products

Set a title, a credit price and how many you have in stock.

03

Issue vouchers

Create a discount voucher with a credit price, a stock count and an optional expiry.

04

Reward good disposal

Citizens log waste they dispose of at your shop and earn credits, which brings them through your door.

What you get

The details.

Free to join

There is no listing fee.

You set the terms

Prices in credits, stock and expiry are yours to decide.

Your own panels

See the shop profile, the vouchers and products you have listed in your dashboard.

Kirana, cafe, recycler or eco store

Any local business can register.

Partner shops are responsible for honouring the products and vouchers they list. See the Terms for shop responsibilities.
