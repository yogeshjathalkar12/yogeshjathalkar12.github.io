City Credits

Create a free account and the reports you file and the waste you dispose of properly earn city credits, kept in your own Basis wallet. Credits are a thank-you for taking part, not a currency.

Create account All features
The problem

Reporting a broken city costs you time and pays you nothing.Credits give something back to the people who do the noticing, and route it to the local shops that make a neighbourhood work.

How it works

Step by step.

01

Create a free account

Email and password, or sign in with Google. Anonymous reporting still works without one.

02

Report issues or log a disposal

File a report with a photo and location, or log waste you dropped off at a partner shop.

03

Credits land in your wallet

Your balance, report count and activity history live in your dashboard.

04

Spend them locally

Use credits in the marketplace or on vouchers from partner shops.

What you get

The details.

Reports

Pothole 8 · Garbage 10 · Sewage 10 · Broken infrastructure 6 · Traffic violation 5 · Littering 3 · Streetlight 5 · Other 3.

Verified disposals

Plastic 4 · E-waste 6 · Organic 3 · Paper 2 · Metal 4 · Glass 3 · Other 2.

Your own wallet

Live balance and full activity history in the dashboard.

Passwordless recovery flow

Forgot your password? A one-time code to your email lets you set a new one.

Credits have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. The amounts shown in your dashboard are the ones that apply, and they may change. Credits can be adjusted or removed where abuse is found. See the Terms.
