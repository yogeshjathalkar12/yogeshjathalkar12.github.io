Why Basis

Complaints are ignored. Evidence is not.

Every city has broken roads, overflowing drains and dead streetlights. What it usually lacks is a way to turn scattered frustration into something an institution has to respond to. That is what Basis is for.

Report an issueSee the features
The problem

One person reporting a pothole is a complaint. A hundred people reporting the same junction is a case.Most reporting channels are built to close tickets, not to show patterns. They do not tell you where problems cluster, how long they last or whether anyone fixed them.

What is different

Four principles.

Anonymous by default

Reporting needs no name, phone number or account. An account is optional and only exists to earn credits.

Evidence, not opinion

Every report is a photo with a location. Photos are checked by a model, then by a person.

Humans stay in the loop

The AI proposes; trusted moderators decide. Nothing trains the model without a person confirming it.

Free, and not for sale

The tools are free to use and your data is not sold. That is a commitment, not a marketing line.
